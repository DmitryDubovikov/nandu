# CLAUDE.md

This file provides guidance to Claude Code (claude.ai/code) when working with code in this repository.

## What this repository is

Not a software project. It is a set of **Russian-language study materials** for preparing a child (10–11 y.o.) for the Argentine math olympiad **Olimpíada Matemática Ñandú, Nivel 1** (regional stage, previously zonal). The "source code" is markdown documents; the "data" is the corpus of original problem statements in `nandu_problemas/`.

Real problems now come straight from `nandu_problemas/omn_regional/regional_<year>.md` (answers: `historico/Regionales_N1.md`; worked №3 by year: `nandu_n1_regional_schet_korpus_resheniya.md`). ⚠ The regional textbooks still contain dangling references to it — they are frozen and must not be edited. `nandu_n1_zonal_roadmap.md` survives and is unrelated.

## Domain model

Every regional-stage paper has exactly 3 problems with the type fixed by position: №1 = Т1 (arithmetic/linear systems), №2 = Т2 (perimeter geometry from a lettered figure), №3 = Т3 (systematic counting). Types split into 8 blocks (1a–1c, 2a–2b, 3a–3c); their definitions lived in the now-deleted regional roadmap, and the block labels survive only as references inside the textbooks and `schet_korpus_resheniya`. Course sequence: СЛАУ → Перевод → Фигуры → Счёт → боевая фаза (working the real bank).

**Corpus finding on №1 techniques (July 2026 audit of ~68 Nivel-1 papers):** «вычти сценарий» (subtracting two near-twin equations) is THE dominant opening move of the regional №1 — 7 of 14 years 2012–2025, including **all five of 2021–2025** (2025 is literally the textbook's `x + y/2` shape); it also appears at zonal (1997, 2002, 2007, 2016, 2023). Drill it to automatism. «Сложи всё» in its pure circular form (`a+b, b+c, a+c` — build the total by adding all equations) has **zero hits** in the №1 Nivel-1 corpus across all stages (zonal 1996–2025, provincial 2000–2025, regional 2012–2025); only its second half (subtract an equation from a *given* total, e.g. zonal 2007, provincial 2025) occurs — and that is again «вычти». The pure circular shape exists in Ñandú but at Tercer Nivel (zonal 2025). So «сложи всё» is taught once as a bridge idea, not drilled. Provincial №1 is a different genre altogether: mostly working-backwards/process problems, almost never dense systems (covered by the doski appendix on working backwards).

## Hard rules when authoring or editing materials

These conventions are load-bearing across all existing pairs — keep them:

1. **The roadmap's problem bank is untouchable.** Real problems from the bank are reserved for the боевая фаза; never copy them (numbers or plots) into textbook examples or reshebnik problems.
2. Textbook examples must not duplicate reshebnik problems, and vice versa — neither in numbers nor in plot.
3. One chapter = one new technique; a problem block's difficulty comes only from that chapter's titular skill; tools only from already-covered chapters.
4. Every reshebnik answer must be computed and independently re-verified (e.g., brute-force enumeration vs. the technique). No unchecked answers.
5. Language is Russian. **Real mathematical terminology is allowed and expected** (July 2026 decision by the user, reversing the earlier ban): «сочетания», «размещения», «перестановки», «факториал», «остаток по модулю», «делимость», «инвариант» and the like may be used as working vocabulary, not just in 🔎 footnotes. The requirement is pedagogical, not lexical: introduce a term the first time it appears — name it, show it on the numbers of a concrete example, and only then use it freely. Existing materials are not to be retro-translated wholesale; new and edited text uses the real terms.
6. Each textbook opens with «ритуалы» (standing habits) and each chapter includes typical mistakes; worked examples follow the step format of rule 8. Match the tone and structure of the existing linsys/perevod pairs.
7. When adding a new topic pair, follow the staged workflow from `combinatorics_v1/nandu_n1_regional_schet_session_prompt.md`: research corpus → derive techniques from the corpus (not from textbooks) → propose chapter ladder + coverage map → **stop for approval** → write the pair; then update the plan/tracker files.
8. **Разборы and composite worked examples are iterative steps with несгораемые артефакты** (июль–авг 2026 decision; supersedes the old «что делаем | что получается» skeleton). Structure: **шаг 1 — план**, ending with a `> ✅ **План готов:** Ответ = …` block — the answer expressed through subtasks. That artifact may be an algebraic combination of blocks («Ответ = Д × М»), a reduction («Ответ = все a, при которых …»), or, for the «какие значения возможны?» genre, **«оценка плюс пример»** («Ответ = те S из списка оценки, для которых нашёлся пример»). Then each subtask is its own bold «Шаг N — …» ending with its result (`→ **Д = 10**`); the last step is сборка — plugging the numbers into the plan's formula. **Every step opens with the goal, not the action**: not «делаю А, потому что Б» but «нужно Б — для этого делаю А» — the red thread must read from the goals alone. A «что делаем | что получается» table as the *skeleton* of a разбор is forbidden (wastes horizontal space, caps cell text, hides the thread); a mini-table or a bullet list of «И»-factors is allowed only *inside* a step for ≥2 short uniform moves, preceded by a goal phrase. Deliberate wrong-way demos («как хочется сделать») may keep the old table look to stay visually distinct. Applies to all new and edited разборы; don't retro-rewrite untouched documents unasked (frozen regional textbooks stay frozen).
9. **Наблюдение, на котором держится приём, называется отдельно** (авг 2026 decision). If a разбор works because of a specific property of the problem (в таблице 2×2 любые две клетки лежат в общей строке или столбце; расстановка одинаковых фишек = выбор дырок; островок одноцветный; условие связывает только соседние строки; одиночка тянет за собой неразличимую пару), that property is stated as its own **«Заметь главное»** paragraph — before шаг 1, or before the step where it first does its work — and is not dissolved into the mechanics of a step. The paragraph answers «почему так вообще можно», the steps answer «как считать». An example whose title promises the observation («одна клетка тянет всю доску») must carry such a paragraph.
10. **Формулы не стоят внутри абзаца** (авг 2026 decision). A formula sitting inside a text line wraps across the line break and has to be read in pieces from two rows — unreadable on the printed page. Put on **its own paragraph**: any expression containing letters, and any chain longer than a single operation. Only a one-operation numeric fact (`3 + 5 = 8`) and a value assignment (`О = 9`) may stay inline. One formula per line — two formulas separated by a comma on one line break so that the tail of the first sticks to the head of the second. Applies to all text, not just разборы (rule 8's «Вёрстка формул» is the same requirement inside a разбор); enforced on new and edited material, checked on the PDF, not on screen.
11. **У боевой записи есть фраза-объяснение** (авг 2026 decision). The child solves fast enough to write the maths but not a prose explanation, and a bare calculation loses the explanation point — Ñandú pays for «Explica cómo las contaste», and between two correct papers the one that argued its move scores higher. So **every non-trivial problem whose material carries a «что пишешь / что достаточно написать на туре»-style record gets a `#### 💬 Фраза-объяснение (в начале)`**: one or two sentences the child writes *before* the calculations — Spanish in bold, Russian translation in italics below it. Counting and enumeration problems (anything ending in «Explica cómo las contaste» / «dar todas las posibilidades») get a second `#### 💬 Фраза в конце — полнота` after the answer. The rest of the record goes under `#### Запись`, and the paragraph in the разбор that the phrase is drawn from is tagged with an italic `💬` line so the source is visible.
    - **Content:** the phrase carries **«почему так вообще можно считать»** — the load-bearing observation of rule 9 («случаи не пересекаются», «общая сторона ушла внутрь», «конец известен точно»). A phrase that retells the arithmetic already visible in the record is a defect, not a phrase.
    - **Perimeters (Т2) get none — deliberate user decision.** There the record speaks for itself: letters, equalities, subtraction. Do not add phrases to them.
    - **The phrases are instances of one closed phrase-book, not per-problem texts.** Thirty separate texts would be thirty memorised strings and nothing transferable to an unseen tour problem; a dozen constructions transfer. The provincial book lives in `nandu_n1_provincial_pamyatka.md`, section «💬 Что писать словами» («когда → что пишешь», by приём). New material reuses those wordings verbatim where the приём matches, and only extends the book when the приём is genuinely new — extending it is a book edit, not a local one. A new branch (national) gets its own phrase-book section in that branch's памятка, seeded from the provincial one.
    - **Budget ≈180 Spanish characters** (two handwritten lines). Language: simple rioplatense at 10–11-year-old level, no term absent from Argentine primary school.
    - **Verification is mandatory and independent** (rule 4's standard): an agent seeing only the problem statement and the record re-solves the problem and judges whether the claim is *true*, whether it carries justification rather than arithmetic, whether the Spanish is natural, and whether it fits the budget. The first pass caught three factually false phrases — this check is not a formality.
    - `grep -rn 💬` lists the whole layer. Applies to new and edited material; don't retro-fit untouched documents unasked (the national branch has no phrases yet).
